Three.js Minigames

Five small browser multiplayer games. Each match runs on its own authoritative Node.js server on Edgegap, started by the Edgegap matchmaker close to the players, and stopped when the match ends.

Shooter

Blaster Arena

Top-down free-for-all. First to 10 kills.

2–8 players · bots fill to 3
 Racing

Kart Rally

Three laps around a bean-shaped circuit. Grass is slow.

1–8 players
 Sports

Rocket Soccer

1v1 or 2v2, teams from the matchmaker. Dash into the ball.

2 teams · bots even them out
 Arcade

Light Cycles

Leave a wall of light; last one riding wins the round.

2–6 players · bots fill to 4
 Party

Sumo Push

Shove everyone off a shrinking ring. Dash for a heavy hit.

2–8 players · bots fill to 4
